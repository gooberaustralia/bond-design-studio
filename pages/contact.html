What should I include in the first message?
The suburb, roughly what you are planning, and what is not working about the house you have now. If you have a budget in mind, say so. Three lines is plenty.
Do you charge for the first conversation?
No. The first conversation is about whether the project makes sense and whether we are the right studio for it.
Which parts of Sydney do you work in?
The Sydney metropolitan area. Residential only.
I have plans already. Can you take them further?
Often yes. Our services can be engaged for selected stages rather than end to end, so an existing concept can be developed, documented and taken through approval.
