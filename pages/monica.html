Lead designer

Monica Vogel

Monica Vogel is an experienced residential designer with a passion for creating homes that are both timeless and deeply personal.

Having worked with respected Sydney practices including Luigi Rosselli Architects and Stafford Architecture, Monica brings extensive experience across bespoke homes, renovations and interior design.

How she works

Careful
listening

Her approach is grounded in thoughtful collaboration, careful listening and an appreciation for the details that make a house feel like home.

Known for her understanding of proportion, materiality and everyday living, Monica works closely with each client to create spaces that are beautiful, functional and uniquely tailored to their lifestyle.

Through Bond Design Studio, she combines high level design expertise with a boutique, personal service, guiding homeowners through every stage of their project with clarity and confidence.

What that means for you

One designer,
start to finish

Unlike larger firms, Bond keeps a highly collaborative relationship with every client. The person you meet at the first site visit is the person who draws your house, sits in the council meeting and answers the builder's questions on site.

That is the whole point of a boutique studio, and it is the reason the studio takes on the work it can genuinely give attention to.

  • Design shaped around one household rather than a catalogue
  • Council and approval guidance from someone who has done it before
  • Coordination with your builder and consultants through documentation
  • Involvement on site, through construction, to the end
