How we work with homeowners

Four ways in. Most projects start as one and turn out to be another, which is why the first conversation is about your house rather than our services.

Our services

We design bespoke homes, rebuilds, renovations and extensions across the Sydney metropolitan area. Residential only, one designer on every project, and involved from the first sketch through to the last site visit.

01  /  Knockdown rebuild

Stay in the street,
change the house

Many homeowners love where they live but find their existing home no longer suits their lifestyle. A knockdown rebuild lets you stay in the location you love while creating a home designed for how you live today. We guide clients through site analysis, concept design, planning approvals, documentation and construction coordination.

Knockdown rebuild design

02  /  Renovations and extensions

Make the house
work again

A well designed renovation can completely change the way a home functions. Whether you are opening living areas, adding bedrooms, improving natural light or creating a stronger connection between indoor and outdoor spaces, we design around the character of your home rather than against it.

Renovation and extension design

03  /  New homes on vacant land

Start with
the block

Building a new home is a rare chance to create something genuinely tailored to your family. From site planning through to detailed design, we work closely with clients to create homes that respond to their block, budget and lifestyle while delivering enduring quality.

New home design

04  /  Interior design

Resolved
with the plan

Great homes are experienced from the inside out. We help clients make cohesive decisions around materials, colours, finishes, fixtures and fittings so the interior reflects the design vision and feels beautifully resolved. It runs alongside the architecture of the house rather than after it, which is why it is part of a full design service rather than something bolted on at the end.
