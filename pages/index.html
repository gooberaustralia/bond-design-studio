What we do

Four ways we work with homeowners

Many homeowners love where they live but find their existing home no longer suits their lifestyle. A knockdown rebuild lets you stay in the location you love while creating a home designed for how you live today.

We guide clients through site analysis, concept design, planning approvals, documentation and construction coordination to create homes that make the most of their land.

Knockdown rebuild in detail

A well designed renovation can completely change the way a home works. Whether you are opening living areas, adding bedrooms, improving natural light or creating a stronger connection between indoor and outdoor spaces, we design around the character of your home rather than against it.

Renovations and extensions in detail

Building a new home is a rare chance to create something genuinely tailored to your family. From site planning through to detailed design, we work closely with clients to create homes that respond to their block, budget and lifestyle while delivering enduring architectural quality.

New homes in detail

Great homes are experienced from the inside out. We help clients make cohesive decisions around materials, colours, finishes, fixtures and fittings so the interior reflects the design vision and feels beautifully resolved.

Common questions

Before you
get in touch

The things homeowners ask us first. If yours is not here, ask it directly and you will get a straight answer.

Do I need an architect or a building designer?
For a standard house in New South Wales, a registered architect is not required. Renovations, extensions and knockdown rebuilds of a single home can be designed and documented by a residential design studio. Architects are required for apartment buildings and other regulated work. What matters more is who you trust with your house, and how far they stay involved.
What does a knockdown rebuild cost in Sydney?
It depends on the size and finish of the new home, and on the block itself. Slope, rock, tree protection, asbestos in the old house and the approval pathway all move the number, sometimes by a lot. We would rather look at your site and give you a real range than quote an average that turns out to be wrong for you.
How do your fees work?
For a complete design service, fees typically commence from 10% plus GST of the construction value, with the final figure set by the scale and complexity of the project. They are invoiced progressively against work completed, and we provide forward estimates so you always know what is coming. You can also engage us for selected stages rather than the whole thing.
Why is that more than a drafting fee?
Because it buys more than drawings. A documentation only fee ends when the plans are printed. Ours covers concept design, design development, full documentation, council and approvals, coordination with your builder and consultants, and involvement through construction so the house that gets built is the house that was designed.
How long does council approval take?
There are two pathways. A complying development certificate is the faster one and suits straightforward sites that meet the standards. A development application goes to your council, takes considerably longer, and is unavoidable on some sites. We work out which one applies to your block early, because it changes the timeline more than anything else.
Can I stay in the house during a renovation?
Sometimes, and it is worth deciding early rather than late. Staging the work so you keep a kitchen and a bathroom is a design decision, not a building one, and it affects the drawings. If moving out is simpler and cheaper overall, we will tell you.
Do you work with my builder, or do you have one?
We are a design studio, not a building company, so you are free to choose your builder. We coordinate with them and with the consultants through documentation and construction. If you do not have a builder yet, we can help you tender the project and compare what comes back properly.
