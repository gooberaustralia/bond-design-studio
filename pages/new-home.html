What we do

Four ways we work with homeowners

Many homeowners love where they live but find their existing home no longer suits their lifestyle. A knockdown rebuild lets you stay in the location you love while creating a home designed for how you live today.

We guide clients through site analysis, concept design, planning approvals, documentation and construction coordination to create homes that make the most of their land.

Talk about your block

A well designed renovation can completely change the way a home works. Whether you are opening living areas, adding bedrooms, improving natural light or creating a stronger connection between indoor and outdoor spaces, we design around the character of your home rather than against it.

Discuss your renovation

Building a new home is a rare chance to create something genuinely tailored to your family. From site planning through to detailed design, we work closely with clients to create homes that respond to their block, budget and lifestyle while delivering enduring architectural quality.

Start with your site

Great homes are experienced from the inside out. We help clients make cohesive decisions around materials, colours, finishes, fixtures and fittings so the interior reflects the design vision and feels beautifully resolved.

How it runs

Concept to
completion

Six stages, in order, with the studio involved through all of them. You always know which stage you are in and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start by understanding your site, your lifestyle, your aspirations and your goals for the project.

  2. 02

    Concept design

    Initial design ideas are explored and refined into a clear vision for your future home.

  3. 03

    Design development

    Layouts, materials and architectural details are developed and coordinated.

  4. 04

    Documentation

    Detailed drawings are prepared for approvals, tendering and construction.

  5. 05

    Builder coordination

    We work with builders and consultants to keep the path from design to construction smooth.

  6. 06

    Construction support

    Our involvement continues on site, protecting the design and helping with key decisions.

Lead designer

Monica Vogel

Monica is an experienced residential designer with a passion for creating homes that are both timeless and deeply personal. Having worked with respected Sydney practices including Luigi Rosselli Architects and Stafford Architecture, she brings extensive experience across bespoke homes, renovations and interior design.

Her approach is grounded in thoughtful collaboration, careful listening and an appreciation for the details that make a house feel like home. Known for her understanding of proportion, materiality and everyday living, Monica works closely with each client to create spaces that are beautiful, functional and uniquely theirs.
