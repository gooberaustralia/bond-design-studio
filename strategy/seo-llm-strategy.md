## 1. The finding that shapes everything

**"Architect" is a protected title in NSW.** ChatGPT's own answer cited
architects.nsw.gov.au/becoming-registered/protection-of-architect-title: only people on the NSW
Register of Architects may call themselves an architect. Monica is a residential designer, not a
registered architect.

Consequences:
- The site must never use "architect", "architectural services" or "architecture studio" as a
  description of Bond or Monica. "Architectural" as an adjective about the quality of the work is
  the grey area; keep it rare and never in a title, H1 or service name.
- Monica having *worked at* Luigi Rosselli Architects and Stafford Architecture is fine and is a
  real asset. Phrase as "worked with" or "at", never "as an architect at".
- The highest-volume Sydney terms are therefore off limits: architect sydney (1,300/mo),
  architecture firms sydney (480), residential architects sydney (140). Do not chase them.
- OPEN: confirm with the client that Monica is not ARB-registered. If she is, this whole section
  inverts and the strategy gets much easier.

### Who won instead
- ChatGPT, "best residential design studios in Sydney for a knockdown rebuild": MILEHAM,
  Hall & Hart, Studio AC & Co, Danny Broe Architect, Durbach Block Jaggers, Allura Homes,
  Meraas, Montgomery Homes.
- Claude, same question: Danny Broe Architect, Sure Space, HXD Built, Managed Build,
  Buildrite Sydney, SLD Projects.
- ChatGPT, "architect or building designer": cited KBG Planning & Design's blog post.

### What the citation pattern actually shows
1. **Builders are winning the designer's question.** ChatGPT split its answer into "architect-led
   practices" and "design-build studios" and filled both with construction companies. There is no
   designer-side answer being given. That is the opening.
2. **The cited pages are dedicated service pages with the phrase in the URL.** Every citation was
   a `/knockdown-rebuild-sydney/` or `/knock-down-rebuild/` page. Not a homepage. Not a portfolio.
3. **Roundup listicles get cited.** buildana.com.au "Best Knockdown Rebuild Builders in Sydney
   (2026)" was a Claude source. Getting into third-party roundups is a real, separate tactic.
4. **A small firm's blog post beat everyone on the explainer question.** KBG Planning & Design, a
   small practice, is what ChatGPT cited for architect vs building designer. That question has no
   dominant owner. Bond can take it.
5. **Cost pages are their own citation economy.** ChatGPT cited five separate cost guides
   (gianta, buildana, buildscopeprojects, bestinsydney, chalkline) for one cost question.

### The fee problem, and it is urgent
ChatGPT currently tells Sydney buyers that building designers charge "~3-5% or a fixed
$10k-$40k", and that architects charge 8-15%. The client's brief says Bond's fees commence from
**10% + GST of construction value**.

So the AI is priming buyers to expect a designer to cost a third of what Bond charges, and to
read 10% as architect pricing from a non-architect. This has to be answered head on, on the
Process/Investment page and in the FAQ, or Bond loses on price framing before the first call.
The honest answer is scope: Bond's 10% is a full end-to-end service through construction, which
is not what a $15k documentation-only draftsperson provides.

## 4. Page-by-page strategy

Content in quotes is the client's own from the creative brief and stays largely as written.

### Home — `/`
- Primary: brand + category. "Bond Design Studio", "residential design studio Sydney".
- Supporting: house designer sydney (20), custom home design sydney (40).
- Title: `Bond Design Studio | Residential Design Studio, Sydney`
- Meta: bespoke homes, knockdown rebuilds, renovations and extensions across Sydney. Led by
  designer Monica Vogel.
- H1: Timeless residential design for the way you live (client's own headline)
- Job of the page: establish calibre, name the four services so the buyer self-identifies, meet
  Monica, one clear action. Already built.
- **Add: FAQ block.** Six questions, answer-first, 40-70 words each. This is the LLM SEO move.
- Schema: ProfessionalService (built) + FAQPage on the new FAQ block.
- Internal links out: all four service pages, Process, Monica, Contact.

**Home FAQ set** (chosen because each maps to a real query cluster or a real AI answer gap):
1. Do I need an architect or a building designer in Sydney? — the KBG-cited gap
2. What does a knockdown rebuild cost in Sydney? — 170/mo cost cluster
3. How much does a designer charge, and what is included? — answers the 3-5% framing head on
4. How long does council approval take? — real buyer anxiety, no volume data, high intent
5. Can I stay in my home during the work? — renovation buyer
6. Do you work with my builder, or do you have one? — the design-build vs designer question ChatGPT raised

  (rebuild / renovation / new home / interiors), budget range, message.
- Suburb and project type on the form are worth more than anything else for qualification.
- Schema: ContactPage.

## 5. Technical and LLM readiness

Already true after the redesign:
- Server rendered static HTML. AI crawlers read raw HTML and execute no JavaScript, so this is
  the right substrate.
- ProfessionalService schema on the home page.
- Absolute canonical, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, llms.txt generated by the build.

To do:
- robots.txt must explicitly allow GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-Web,
  PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended. Check what stitch emits.
- FAQPage schema wherever FAQ content exists, and only where it genuinely exists.
- Service schema per service page.
- Person schema on Monica.
- business.canonical_url is empty in the build config, so canonicals and sitemap loc values come
  out relative and invalid. Fix before launch.
- Google Business Profile. OPEN: does Bond have one. Without it the local pack and a large part
  of AI local grounding are unavailable.
