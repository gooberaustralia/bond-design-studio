Our Services

What We Do

At Bond Design Studio, we offer a comprehensive range of services to help you create your perfect home. Our approach is collaborative, ensuring that every step of the design process is in line with your vision.

Custom Home Design

We collaborate with you from the very first sketch to the final detail — crafting a home that reflects your personality, lifestyle, and long-term vision. Every decision is considered. Nothing is incidental.

Interior Design

From spatial planning and material selection to lighting and furniture, we design interiors that are harmonious, functional, and enduring. Spaces that feel as good as they look.

Home Transformations

We see the potential in existing homes. Whether a single room or a whole-home redesign, we bring fresh eyes and careful thinking to spaces that deserve a new chapter.

Sustainable Design

Beautiful design and responsible practice are not in opposition. We integrate sustainable materials, passive principles, and considered detailing to create homes that tread lightly and last.

Lead Designer

Monica Vogel

Monica founded Bond Design Studio on a single conviction: that a home should be an honest expression of the life lived in it. With over two decades of practice across Sydney's most thoughtful residential projects, she has developed a design language defined by restraint, craft, and clarity.

Her training in architecture and her enduring interest in the way people inhabit space inform every project — from the first meeting to the day the keys are handed over. Monica has been recognised by the Australian Institute of Architects and her work has been published in Vogue Living, Belle, and Architectural Digest Australia.

She believes the quietest rooms are often the most memorable.
